🎨 Chinese Prompt

主题：
晨花淡紫和服人像

主体：
画面中央，一位年轻女性坐在有花朵和朝阳的和洋室沙发上。她穿着白色底、淡紫花纹的和服，系紫色腰带，左下方伸出一条腿，侧脸和胸口为主视觉。

人物与表情：
细长卵形脸，小巧下巴，低垂的淡褐色眼睛，细眉，清秀鼻梁，略微张开的红桃色唇。脸朝画面左下方，神情安静，目光看向左肩指尖。明亮棕色长发在后脑勺松散束起，配薄刘海和脸侧发束，右后方有白色小花发饰。

服装与姿势：
和服从双肩大幅滑落，底色由白到浅紫，散布紫、青、淡桃色菊花和小花，胸口深V敞开。腰间系浓淡紫色织纹腰带，中央有珍珠风花朵装饰。人物斜坐在沙发上，左腿向前伸展，右臂支撑身体，左手指尖触碰左肩领口。

背景与光线：
画面左后方有大木框窗户，透明花瓶中插着白与紫的花朵，旁边有水杯；画面右后方有木制梳妆台、圆镜、玻璃瓶和和纸灯罩的灯。白色长毛沙发与花纹靠垫。左窗射入柔和晨光，照亮脸庞、肩膀、和服与前方腿部。

构图与镜头：
3:4纵向构图，从略高于坐姿人物胸部的正面左侧镜头取景，收录从头顶到前方大腿中央。人物置于中央偏右，左腿位于左下方，窗户和花朵位于左上方。腿部和右臂在画面边缘裁切，侧脸与和服花纹清晰对焦，背景柔和虚化。

质感与风格：
照片般的真实写实风格，自然肌肤、光泽头发、和服织纹与淡雅花样、腰带、珍珠装饰、白色长毛沙发细节清晰，整体为白色、浅紫、木色的明亮细腻晨光。

负面：
肩膀被和服完全覆盖；改变淡紫花纹与坐姿。

🎨 English Prompt

Theme:
Morning floral lavender kimono portrait

Subject:
A young woman sits on a sofa in a Japanese-Western room with flowers and morning sunlight. She wears a white kimono with pale lavender floral patterns and a purple obi, with one leg extending toward the lower left; the side face and chest are the main focus.

Appearance and expression:
A slender oval face, small chin, downcast light brown eyes, thin eyebrows, a neat nose bridge, and slightly parted glossy red-peach lips. Her face turns toward the lower left of the frame, with a calm expression looking at the fingertips near her left shoulder. Her long bright brown hair is loosely tied at the back of her head, with thin bangs and face-framing strands, plus a small white flower hair ornament at the back right.

Clothing and pose:
The kimono-like dress is largely slipped off both shoulders, with a base color shifting from white to light lavender and scattered with purple, blue, and pale pink chrysanthemums and small flowers; the chest is deeply open in a deep V. A woven obi in layered purple tones is tied at the waist, with a pearl-like flower ornament in the center. She sits diagonally on the sofa, stretching her left leg forward, supporting her body with her right arm, while the fingertips of her left hand touch the collar at her left shoulder.

Background and light:
A large wooden-framed window is in the left rear, with a transparent vase holding white and purple flowers and a water cup nearby; in the right rear are a wooden dresser, round mirror, glass bottles, and a paper-shade lamp. A white long-pile sofa and floral cushions are present. Soft morning sunlight from the left window lights the face, shoulders, kimono, and front leg.

Composition and camera:
Vertical 3:4 composition, shot from a slightly higher front-left angle than the seated subject’s chest, framing from the top of the head to the middle of the front thigh. Place the subject large in the center-right, the left leg in the lower left, and the window and flowers in the upper left. Crop the leg and right arm at the frame edge, keep the side face and kimono floral pattern in focus, and softly blur the background.

Texture and style:
Photorealistic real-life photography style, with natural skin, glossy hair, kimono weave and delicate floral patterns, obi, pearl ornament, and the white long-pile sofa rendered in high detail, under bright and delicate morning light in white, light purple, and wood tones.

Negative:
A kimono fit that fully covers the shoulders; changing the pale lavender floral pattern and seated pose.
