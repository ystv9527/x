📝 Summary

以瑞士国际主义网格、磨砂玻璃分层和身体碎片化为核心的极简文化节海报提示词，适合角色匿名化再设计。

🎨 Chinese Prompt

瑞士现代主义 × 磨砂玻璃观念摄影 × 身体碎片化 × 极简文化节海报。

只保留角色最核心的身体轮廓或极少量能够辨识身份的剪影特征，不直接展示清晰五官和完整服装，将角色转换为匿名化的观念人体影像。

角色站在一整块乳白半透明磨砂玻璃或树脂板后方，身体保持近似直立，整体轮廓沿画面纵向延伸。让角色大部分身体与介质保持一定距离，因此形成宽阔、柔软、完全失焦的人形暗影；同时随机选择头部、手臂、手掌、膝部或腿部中的少量局部靠近或贴住磨砂表面，使这些区域从模糊剪影中转化成深黑、细长、清晰度更高的身体碎片。不要依靠五官、服装细节或直接角色展示建立画面，而是通过人体轮廓和局部形态表达角色存在。

采用竖版约2:3构图，角色主轴固定在中央偏左或中央区域，周围保留约一半以上大面积负空间。使用接近50—85mm的自然至中长焦视觉，中距离、眼平机位拍摄，不制造夸张广角透视。空间固定为“镜头—磨砂介质—角色”三层结构，以前景半透明板、局部清晰身体碎片、后方模糊人体剪影构成深度。

使用角色后方的大面积漫射背光，让光线穿过空间并被人体遮挡，再经过磨砂表面产生宽阔柔化阴影。整体保持高键奶灰白和浅青灰背景，角色局部压成近黑色，仅加入约5%的高纯度柠檬黄色作为视觉焦点。

版式固定采用瑞士国际主义网格设计：在角色旁边设置小型日期、地点、编号和文化活动说明文字，以极简无衬线字体形成清晰信息层级；一个短主标题使用柠檬黄色大字号排版，其余文字保持黑、白或浅灰。保持文字极锐利、摄影主体极柔化的质感冲突，并维持大面积留白、非对称平衡、低视觉噪音与印刷海报感，最终形成瑞士现代主义 × 半透明介质观念摄影 × 身体碎片化 × 极简文化节海报的稳定视觉体系。

🎨 English Prompt

Swiss modernism × frosted-glass conceptual photography × fragmented body × minimalist cultural festival poster.

Keep only the character’s core body contour or a very small number of silhouette features that can identify the subject. Do not directly show clear facial features or complete clothing. Transform the character into an anonymized conceptual human image.

Place the character behind a single sheet of milky, semi-transparent frosted glass or resin board, with the body kept nearly upright and the overall contour extending vertically through the frame. Keep most of the body at a distance from the translucent surface so it becomes a broad, soft, fully defocused human shadow. At the same time, randomly let a few parts such as the head, arms, hands, knees, or legs approach or touch the frosted surface, turning those areas from blurry silhouette into darker, narrower, sharper body fragments. Do not build the image through facial details, clothing details, or a direct character reveal; express the character’s presence through body contour and localized form.

Use a vertical 2:3 composition, with the main axis of the character placed near the center-left or center, leaving more than half the frame as large negative space. Use a natural to medium telephoto look of about 50 to 85mm, shot from a mid-distance, eye-level camera position, without exaggerated wide-angle perspective. Keep the space as a three-layer structure of camera, frosted medium, and character, creating depth with a translucent foreground panel, localized sharp body fragments, and a blurred human silhouette in the back.

Use large diffuse backlight behind the character so the light passes through the space, is blocked by the body, and then creates broad softened shadows through the frosted surface. Keep the overall palette high-key with milky gray white and pale cyan gray backgrounds, compress the character into near-black local forms, and add only about 5 percent high-saturation lemon yellow as the visual focal point.

Use a Swiss International Style grid layout: place small date, location, number, and cultural event copy beside the character in a minimalist sans-serif type hierarchy; set a short main title in large lemon-yellow type, while the remaining text stays black, white, or light gray. Maintain the contrast between extremely sharp text and very soft photographic subject, with large blank space, asymmetrical balance, low visual noise, and a printed poster feel, forming a stable visual system of Swiss modernism × translucent-medium conceptual photography × body fragmentation × minimalist cultural festival poster.
