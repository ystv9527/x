📝 Summary

A Chinese prompt template for generating Cornell note-style learning infographics with GPT-Image, suitable for knowledge summary pages and textbook-style layouts.

🎨 Chinese Prompt

请设计一张高质量的信息图，采用「康奈尔笔记」版式，用于学习【主题】。

整体要求：A4竖版，4K高清，扁平化设计，清爽、干净、现代；白色背景，马卡龙配色（蓝、绿、橙），适合打印和电子阅读；中文字体清晰，留有充足留白，整体风格类似优秀学习资料、Notion、GoodNotes。

整体布局：顶部包含标题（康奈尔笔记）、日期、科目、课题。左侧约25%宽度为线索栏（提问），放置8~12个与主题相关的问题，编号排列。右侧约75%宽度为笔记栏（记录），按知识点模块化排版；每个知识点包含小标题、简洁解释、项目符号、流程图、箭头关系、表格、示意图和图标，要求图文结合，不要只有文字。底部为总结栏（回顾），用2~5句话总结整页内容；右下角增加一句话记忆，用最容易记住的一句话概括整个知识点。

内容要求：围绕【主题】进行知识整理，内容准确、层次清晰、重点突出、易于复习，像一本高质量教材。

视觉元素：适当加入书本、灯泡、笔记、流程图、网络图、模块框、箭头、勾选、重点、目标等图标，且图标统一风格。

排版要求：大量模块化、大量留白、边框圆角、浅色分区、每个知识点单独一个模块、字体大小具有层级。最终效果要像一本大学教材中的学习总结页，又像B站百万播放课程里的知识总结，具有专业信息图质感。

🎨 English Prompt

Please design a high-quality infographic in a Cornell Notes layout for studying [Topic].

Overall requirements: A4 portrait, 4K ultra-high resolution, flat design, clean, fresh, and modern; white background, pastel colors (blue, green, orange), suitable for printing and digital reading; clear Chinese typography, plenty of white space, and a style similar to excellent study materials, Notion, and GoodNotes.

Layout: The top area should include the title (Cornell Notes), date, subject, and topic. The left side, about 25% of the width, is the cue column (questions), with 8 to 12 topic-related questions numbered in order. The right side, about 75% of the width, is the notes column (records), arranged in a modular layout by knowledge point; each knowledge point should include a subheading, concise explanation, bullet points, flowcharts, arrow relationships, tables, diagrams, and icons. The design must combine text and visuals, not text only. The bottom area is the summary section (review), with 2 to 5 sentences summarizing the page; add a one-sentence memory note in the bottom right to describe the whole topic in the easiest way to remember.

Content requirements: organize knowledge around [Topic], with accurate content, clear hierarchy, highlighted key points, easy review, and the feel of a high-quality textbook.

Visual elements: appropriately include icons such as books, light bulbs, notes, flowcharts, network diagrams, module boxes, arrows, check marks, highlights, and goals, all in a consistent icon style.

Typesetting requirements: strong modular structure, plenty of whitespace, rounded borders, light-colored sections, each knowledge point in its own module, and clear font hierarchy. The final result should feel like a study summary page from a university textbook, and also like a knowledge summary from a highly viewed Bilibili course, with professional infographic quality.
